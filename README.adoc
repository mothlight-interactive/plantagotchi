= Plantagotchi

A pixel-art Tamagotchi for a plant. Keep it alive by giving it light, water, fertilizer and love.
The scene mirrors the real weather and time of day at your location; rain waters the plant automatically.

A Mothlight Interactive game. Originally built as the ws6C Mobile Experience project (FHNW) by Joyce Hänggi
and Florian Thiévent, now a Kotlin/Compose Multiplatform app for Android and iOS.

== Project structure

[cols="1,3"]
|===
|`shared/` |Kotlin Multiplatform module: game logic, data layer, the whole Compose UI and resources
|`shared/src/androidMain` |Light sensor and fused location (Play Services)
|`shared/src/iosMain` |CoreLocation, `MainViewController` entry point
|`androidApp/` |Android application (`dev.mothlight.plantagotchi`)
|`iosApp/` |Xcode project, SwiftUI host for the Compose UI
|`supabase/migrations/` |Database schema for cloud save
|===

== Build

Android:

[source,bash]
----
./gradlew :androidApp:assembleDebug
----

iOS: open `iosApp/iosApp.xcodeproj` in Xcode and run. Set your team in `iosApp/Configuration/Config.xcconfig`
or in a non-committed `iosApp/Configuration/Secrets.xcconfig` (`TEAM_ID=...`).

Tests:

[source,bash]
----
./gradlew :shared:testAndroidHostTest
----

== Cloud save (Supabase, optional)

Without configuration the game is stored on the device only. To sync it with Supabase:

. Apply all `supabase/migrations/*.sql` to your project, in order.
. Enable *Allow anonymous sign-ins* (Authentication > Sign In / Providers).
. Add to `local.properties` (not committed):
+
----
SUPABASE_URL=https://<project-ref>.supabase.co
SUPABASE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY=sb_publishable_...
----

The state is always written locally and mirrored to Supabase; on start the most recent copy wins.

== Game rules

* Up to 4 plants: *Sprout*, *Cactus* (little water, loves sun, hates frost) and *Fern* (likes shade, drinks a lot).
  Switch between them with the arrows next to the pot.
* *Light*: rises while the ambient light is above 1000 lux, otherwise empties in about a day.
  Android uses the light sensor. iOS has no public light sensor API: light is estimated from daylight and
  weather, or measured with the camera if enabled in the settings.
* *Water*: refilled by rain, otherwise empties in about two days. Friends can send water once a day.
* *Love*: swipe over the plant to pet it, empties in a week.
* *Fertilizer*: empties in two weeks and speeds up growth.
* *Temperature*: above 30 °C plants dry out faster, below 0 °C all needs drop faster (cacti suffer most).
* *Growth*: healthy plants (average need ≥ 60 %) grow from seedling to young plant (6 h) to grown (48 h).
* *Withering*: below 10 % health for 24 hours a plant withers. Revive it in the shop, replant it for free or compost it.
* *Daily chest*: coins once a day, more for consecutive days (streak).
* Every gain earns a coin. Decay, growth and withering continue while the app is closed.
* Seasons follow the date and hemisphere: snow in winter, flowers and butterflies in spring and summer,
  falling leaves in autumn.

== Shop

Tap the shop bag, the coin counter or the water/fertilizer bubble.

* *Watering can* (15 coins): +35 water
* *Fertilizer* (25 coins): +50 fertilizer
* *Grow lamp* (60 coins): full light for 30 minutes, stacks
* *Revival tonic* (120 coins): brings a withered plant back
* *Seeds* (100 to 150 coins): a new plant
* *Pots* (80 to 200 coins): 12 pots in 3 shapes and 4 colors

== More

* *Friends* (needs Supabase): share your 6 character friend code, see friends' plants and send them water.
* *Reminders*: local notifications when a need will drop below 20 % and when the daily chest is ready.
* *Widgets*: Android home screen widget (Glance). The iOS WidgetKit code is in `iosApp/PlantagotchiWidget`,
  see its README to add the extension target.
* *Sound*: 8-bit effects, synthesized and stored in `composeResources/files/sfx`.
* *Languages*: English and German.
* *Weather*: tap the weather chip for details.

== Rendering

The game scene is a single Compose `Canvas` (`shared/src/commonMain/.../ui/game`) redrawn every frame:

* `GameCanvas`: frame loop, input and hit testing, draws plant, pot, HUD and need bubbles
* `SkyRenderer`: sky, sun and moon, stars, fog, lightning, clouds and precipitation
* `SceneState`: animation clock, effects (hearts, floating coins, water, dirt, sparkles, falling leaves) and tool animations
* `PlantRenderer`: plants are drawn procedurally on a pixel grid, no images. Stem, leaves, cactus arms,
  fern fronds and flowers come from a skeleton, so plants grow continuously (leaves unfold, arms grow out,
  fronds unroll from fiddleheads, flowers open) and every part can move. Each plant's shape is seeded by its id.
* `PlantAnimator`: wind bend and leaf flutter, plus idle actions every few seconds (wiggle, stretch, turn to the sun,
  shake off rain, dance when loved, nod at night) and reactions to petting, watering, growing and withering
* `PixelSprite`: other pixel art defined in code (watering can, fertilizer bag, grow lamp, shop bag)
* `MothlightLogo`: the pixel version of the Mothlight wordmark with a glowing lamp and a circling moth (splash and about screen)

Neglected plants let their leaves hang, lose color and drop leaves; withered plants collapse. Low needs pulse and shake. Fireflies appear on calm nights.
